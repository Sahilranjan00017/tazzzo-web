## Product findings (recorded, not fixed)

There are no new product bugs. Both findings from 2026-10-08 are still present on the merged heads:

1. **The app's product-id grammar is narrower than everyone else's.**
   - `tazzzo-app` `c5caed0` `ContentModels.kt:37,111` accepts only `^TZP-[0-9]+$`.
   - Backend `main` (`ContentBlock.java:152,154`), CMS `main` (`apps/admin/src/lib/home-content.ts:62,66`) and the storefront (`apps/storefront/src/lib/ids.ts:8`) all accept `TZP-[A-Za-z0-9-]{1,40}`.
   - Repro and impact are unchanged; see `EVIDENCE_2026-10-08.md`. The rule was checked in the source, not exercised at runtime (the seeded ids are numeric). It needs a ruling on which grammar is canonical.
2. **The storefront banner placeholder is not centred.** `.banner__media { display:block }` (`globals.css:199-201`) overrides `.img-fallback { display:flex }` (`globals.css:170-177`). It is visible again in `screens/t14-website-home-cdn-down.png`, where the mark sits top-left.
